# Redesigning Psychiatry's Sensory Room
### This is the official control system designed for the nurses and the authorised staff to operate and monitor the sensory room. This is a very useful for patients that have specific disability diagnosis and they can choose their preferred sensory preferences for their enjoyable experience in a safe, calming environment. Depending on what their disability is, the system can automatically give them a preferrable setting for each feature, but they are free to change the settings as a patient. It is used to log in patients usage time. Save their preferences and prepare the room before they arrive if required. It has a detailed feedback form for the patients that can improve their future experiences with the psychiatric department of the sensory room.
